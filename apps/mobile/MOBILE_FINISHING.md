## Native limitations and freeze decision

**BLOCKED:** `pnpm nx run mobile:android-build` still stops at `JAVA_HOME is not set and no 'java' command could be found in your PATH.` Java/JDK, Android SDK, adb, emulator and a native device tool are unavailable. No actual Android screenshots were captured.

Unverified cases: Android safe-area/system-bar dimensions, physical touch targets and icons, device font scaling, native keyboard open/close and focused-field/CTA/toast reachability, native dropdown/date/time dialogs and bottom sheets, TalkBack, hardware/gesture back and the discard prompt. The browser passes and Android export do not establish those cases. iOS runtime is also unverified.

The brief loading badge in some browser captures was traced to Expo’s `node_modules/expo/src/environment/DevLoadingView.web.tsx` and its `__expo_fast_refresh` element; it was left intact. No RevuPing floating gear was found. Without the original native screenshot/device, that gear’s exact origin cannot be established; no speculative removal was made.

**The design is ready to freeze based on the available checks, with native acceptance pending.** This pass ends here; no redesign or backend work follows.
