Scene prompts requested warm daylight, independent businesses, natural customer conversations, sage/lavender accents, no text or brands. The portrait prompt requested an invented man, plain clothing, natural skin texture and a neutral background, without resembling a recognizable real person.

On-page descriptions identify illustrative scenes and fictional examples. Shared `Avatar` requires an explicit `demoPortrait` opt-in; real accounts use initials or their own uploads. No user-avatar service was added. Images have intrinsic dimensions, responsive crops and meaningful scene alt text; redundant decorative demo portraits use empty alt text beside their labels.

The composition refinement preserves all three WebP files unchanged. Display styles apply mild desaturation and a six-percent navy overlay to the business scenes, crop the auth image to 240px on desktop (220px on narrower screens), and pair the single landing florist scene with the existing fictional feedback example. Feedback-card overlap is limited to 18px at the lower image edge and removed on mobile.
